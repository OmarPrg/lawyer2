# Client Questionnaire for Law Firm Website Development

This document contains a comprehensive list of questions and information you should request from a client when starting a new law firm website project, based on the structure and design system of our current project.

## 1. General Business Information
*   **Firm Identity:** What is the official name of the law firm as it should appear on the website?
*   **Core Focus:** What are the primary legal services or practice areas you specialize in? 
*   **Target Audience:** Who is your ideal client? (e.g., individuals, corporations, specific industries, local vs. international).
*   **Competitive Edge:** What makes your firm unique? Why should a client choose you over your competitors? (e.g., years of experience, specific track record, personalized approach).
*   **Website Goals:** What is the primary objective of the new website? (e.g., generating phone calls, booking consultations, establishing brand authority, providing educational resources).

## 2. Branding, Design & Aesthetics
*   **Existing Assets:** Do you have a current logo, brand guidelines, or marketing materials? If yes, please provide them.
*   **Color Palette:** We typically recommend a modern, luxurious, and professional color palette (e.g., deep navy blue, gold accents, white, and soft grays). Are you comfortable with this direction, or do you have specific brand colors we must use?
*   **Visual Style:** The proposed style is modern, minimalist, and trustworthy with smooth gradients. Does this align with your firm's image?
*   **Typography:** The website will be primarily in Arabic. Do you have a preferred Arabic font (e.g., Cairo, Tajawal), or should we use our recommended premium fonts?
*   **Imagery:** Do you have high-quality, professional photographs of your team, offices, and partners? If not, should we utilize premium stock photography or arrange a professional photoshoot?

## 3. Website Structure & Features
Based on our standard high-converting structure, please provide input on the following sections:

*   **Hero Section (First Impression):** What is the main headline or slogan you want visitors to see immediately? What should the primary Call-To-Action (CTA) button say? (e.g., "Request a Free Consultation", "Contact Us on WhatsApp").
*   **About Us:** Please provide the firm's history, mission, vision, and information about the founding partners or key lawyers.
*   **Services Portfolio:** We will create individual pages for each service. Please list all services you want to feature. Do you have detailed explanations or previous case studies/articles for each service?
*   **Why Choose Us (Value Proposition):** Please list 3 to 4 key reasons clients trust you (e.g., 24/7 Availability, 20+ Years Experience, High Success Rate).
*   **Testimonials (Client Reviews):** Do you have positive reviews or success stories from past clients that we can display on the site?
*   **Contact Information:** Please provide your office address(es), phone numbers, email addresses, working hours, and any social media links (LinkedIn, Twitter, etc.). Do you want a WhatsApp direct-chat button?

## 4. Content & Copywriting (Arabic)
*   **Content Readiness:** Do you have all the Arabic text prepared for the website pages, or will you need professional legal copywriting services?
*   **Articles/Insights:** Do you plan to regularly publish legal articles or news related to your services to improve SEO and authority? Who will be responsible for writing these?

## 5. Technical & Functional Requirements
*   **Language:** The primary language is Arabic (RTL layout). Do you require a bilingual website (e.g., Arabic and English) in the future?
*   **Content Management:** Will you need an easy-to-use admin panel (CMS) to add new services, articles, or testimonials yourself, or will we handle all updates?
*   **Booking/Forms:** Do you need an automated appointment scheduling system integrated, or is a standard contact form with email notifications sufficient?
*   **Third-party Integrations:** Do you use any CRM software, email marketing tools, or analytics tools that need to be connected to the website?
*   **Domain & Hosting:** Do you already own a domain name (e.g., www.yourlawfirm.com) and web hosting, or do you need us to set this up for you?

## 6. Project Logistics & Timeline
*   **Point of Contact:** Who will be the primary person responsible for providing feedback, content, and final approvals during the project?
*   **Timeline:** Do you have a strict deadline or a specific launch date in mind?
*   **Budget:** Has a specific budget been allocated for the design, development, and potential copywriting/photography of this project?
